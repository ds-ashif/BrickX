🧱 Online Brick Selling & Revenue Generation System
📌 Overview
This project is an online platform for selling bricks, designed to streamline orders, manage inventory, and track revenue generation efficiently.

🚀 Features
🏗 Online Ordering – Customers can browse and purchase bricks.
📦 Inventory Management – Real-time tracking of stock levels.
💰 Revenue Analytics – Insights into sales and earnings.
